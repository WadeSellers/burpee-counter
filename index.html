Counting

  • Space or tap anywhere — count one rep. Rep 1 starts the clock.
  • Every rep lands with the jelly animation. O or Options — pick another move (flips, drops, spins, slot scrambles...) or go Random. Saved on this device.
  • Options also sets the DAY badge date and the end card phrases
  • Voice on: say the rep number ("fourteen") and the counter jumps to it. Missed one? The next number you say resyncs it.

Timer

  • S or say "stop" / "pause" — freeze the clock
  • S again or say "start" — resume (also starts the clock before rep 1)
  • The screen stays awake while the clock runs — no mid-set dimming

Reset

  • R or the Reset button — count and clock back to zero

Milestones

  • Every 10 — gold sparks, keeps counting
  • 25, 50, 75 — screen flips for a moment (taps still count)
  • 100 — finale locks your time, then the follow/like/share loop runs until you tap or press a key
  • E — jump straight to that loop anytime, for recording a clean end card clip

Voice

  • V or the Voice button — mic on/off. Red dot = listening.
  • Works best in Chrome with internet. Speak numbers clearly, not whispered.
